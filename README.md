# Quote Ready

Done-for-you first quote, plus a follow-up the owner sends about three days later. Operator: Kenneth Farrell, Ireland. Not VAT registered. Written 2 October 2026.

This folder does not email a customer, does not take payment, and does not invent a price.

The contact address for this service is farrellfieldnotes@agentmail.to. Do not put a personal Gmail address on the public page.

## What it is

Quote Ready is for an Irish trade or a small agency with one real enquiry. The paid work, when payment exists, is two drafts:

1. A first quote the owner sends from their own email.
2. A short follow-up written to be sent by the owner about three days after they sent the quote, if they still want to send it.

€79 once per enquiry. A second enquiry is a second job.

Payment is not live. A way to pay is added when the first job is taken. Do not ask for card details. Do not invent bank details.

## What a complete intake is

`intake.html` builds `case.txt` in the browser and downloads it. It does not post the form.

Required before the one-working-day clock starts:

- Owner name, business name, and the email they will send from
- Trade and town
- Their customer’s name
- What was asked for
- The prices the owner already uses

If a price, an inclusion, or a payment term is missing, leave it as not supplied. Do not look up a “going rate”. Do not add VAT. Kenneth is not VAT registered; do not add a VAT line unless the owner wrote one themselves.

## How a job runs

1. Read `case.txt` and `handover.md`.
2. Copy `handover.md` and replace every `[bracket]` from the case file only.
3. Show the filled quote and the filled follow-up to the owner. Change only what they ask, if it stays inside the facts they supplied.
4. Show the exact text, the recipient, and the channel to Kenneth.
5. Stop. The owner sends from their own email, and only after Kenneth has approved that exact message. The owner’s yes is also required. It is not a substitute for Kenneth’s.

A template, this README, or a standing instruction is not approval.

Do not email the customer “to check the address”. Do not send the follow-up because three days passed. The owner decides, on the day, whether they have had a reply.

## What Quote Ready will not do

- Invent prices, materials, durations, or guarantees.
- Pretend to be a solicitor, a court, or the tradesperson in a way that hides who is writing. The sender is the owner.
- Threaten proceedings or imply a legal deadline that the owner did not state.
- Promise the quote will be accepted, or promise any ranking or marketing result.
- File anything, chase an unpaid invoice, or edit a Google listing. Those are separate services and are not sold from this folder.
- Take the customer’s payment. If the enquiry is accepted, the owner is paid on the terms they supplied.

## How long

One working day from a complete intake. A working day is Monday to Friday, Ireland, excluding Irish public holidays. The output that day is a draft, not a sent email.
