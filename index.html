Quote Ready · Ireland

A first quote, and the follow-up three days later, written for you to send.

Quote Ready is for Irish trades and small agencies. You get one quote for one enquiry, plus a short follow-up meant for three days later. You send both from your own email. €79 once. Nothing is sent to your customer until Kenneth has approved that exact message.

Start with the intake Email farrellfieldnotes@agentmail.to

The intake stays in your browser and downloads a file named case.txt. It does not email your customer and it does not take payment.

Who it is for

  • A trade or a small agency in Ireland that has a real enquiry and wants the first reply written.
  • Someone who already knows their prices. The draft uses your figures. It does not invent a rate.
  • An owner who will send the quote, and the follow-up, from their own email after the wording is agreed.

Who it is not for

  • A public tender, a large specification, or a multi-site contract. This is one enquiry, one quote, one follow-up.
  • Someone who wants a price made up because they have not decided their rates. Missing figures stay blank for you to fill.
  • A site survey, a measured drawing, or a visit. The draft is written from what you type.
  • Legal terms, a contract, or a guarantee drafted as legal advice. You can state exclusions in your own words. Quote Ready is not a solicitor.
  • Unpaid invoices, debt chasing, or a Google listing. This page is only the quote and the follow-up.
  • Anyone who wants the message to pretend to be from a solicitor, a court, or a regulator.

Exactly what you get

  1. One quote message you can paste into your own email. It states what you were asked to do, the line items and totals you supplied, what is included, what is not included, how you want to be paid, and how long the price stands.
  2. One follow-up message, written to be sent by you about three days after the quote, if you have not had a reply and you still want to send it. It refers back to the same job and the same total. It does not add a new price or a threat.
  3. Both drafts shown to you for corrections, then held until Kenneth has approved the exact text. You send them. Quote Ready is not the sender.

The three days are counted by you from the day you actually send the quote. Quote Ready does not watch your inbox and does not send the follow-up on a timer.

What you send

The intake asks for:

  • Your name and your business name
  • Your email, the one you will send the quote from
  • Your trade, or what the agency does, and your town
  • Your customer’s name, and their email if you already have it
  • What they asked for, in plain words
  • Your prices, one line each, with quantities if you know them
  • What is included, and what is not
  • How you want to be paid, in your words
  • How many days the price stands
  • An optional note

Submit downloads case.txt on your computer. Email that file to farrellfieldnotes@agentmail.to. The form does not send it to your customer. Do not put their card number or your bank login in the note. If you want the quote to name a bank account, put only the details you already give customers.

What Quote Ready will not do

  • No price, material, or timescale you did not supply. No “going rate”, and no claim that the quote will be accepted.
  • No email or text to your customer until Kenneth has approved that exact message. Your own yes is also required. You send it from your own email.
  • No legal threats, no court claims, and no pretend solicitor wording in the quote or the follow-up.
  • No ranking promise and no ads. This is not a marketing retainer.
  • No payment taken on this page. Your customer’s payment, if they accept, goes to you on the terms you wrote.

Price

€79 once, for one enquiry: the quote and the three-day follow-up. A different enquiry is a new job.

Payment is not live. A way to pay is added when the first job is taken. Until then, start by email or the intake. VAT is not added: the operator is not VAT registered.

How long it takes

One working day from a complete intake. A working day is Monday to Friday in Ireland, not an Irish public holiday. Complete means your name, business, email, the customer’s name, what they asked for, and at least the prices you want used are in the file. If a price is missing, that line is sent back blank the same working day for you to fill, rather than guessed. The day produces drafts. It does not send them.

Worked example

Example only · not a customer · prices were supplied by the fictional owner · nothing was sent

Sam Doyle, fictional, trades as Red Gate Carpentry in Ennis. A fictional household, the Hayes family, asked for three internal doors supplied and hung. Sam’s intake gave these figures, which are not a recommended rate: hang a door €80 each, supply a door €120 each, take away the old doors €40. Included: supply and hang three doors, hinges as Sam normally fits. Not included: painting, new frames, and making good plaster. Price stands 21 days. Payment on completion, by bank transfer to the account Sam already uses. Total €640.

Subject: Quote — three internal doors — Red Gate Carpentry

Hello, thanks for asking about three internal doors. The price is €640: supply three doors at €120 each (€360), hang three doors at €80 each (€240), and take away the old doors (€40). That includes the hinges I normally fit. It does not include painting, new frames, or making good plaster. The price stands for 21 days. If you want the work, reply to this email and I will confirm a day. Payment is on completion, by bank transfer to the account I will send with the confirmation. This quote is from Sam Doyle, Red Gate Carpentry.

Follow-up, for Sam to send about three days later, only if there has been no reply:

Hello, I sent a quote on [the day Sam actually sent it] for three internal doors, total €640. It stands for 21 days from that quote. Tell me if you want the work, or if the job has changed. Sam Doyle, Red Gate Carpentry.

Sam would send both from Sam’s own email, and only after Sam approved the wording and Kenneth approved that exact message. These names and prices are made up for this page.
